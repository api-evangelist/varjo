The full Eurofighter Typhoon cockpit on the ADAMS stand drew a crowd for two days at DSET in Cheltenham and took the show's Best Exhibit Award. Inside it: a 1:1 replica cockpit from Viper Wing, Metrea's NOR simulation environment, and a Varjo XR-4 Secure Edition. ADAMS has supported academic training for German Air Force Eurofighter and Tornado aircrew for more than twenty years from its base in Wittmund.
