A technical support case at Varjo rarely looks like a consumer one. The headset in question is usually installed in a certified simulator, on an air base or at a training center, with a syllabus running around it. There may be three organizations involved before you even reach us: a prime contractor, a system integrator, and the unit that actually trains on the thing.
