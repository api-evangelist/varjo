# Varjo - Mission-ready XR for training and simulation 

> Varjo builds high-fidelity VR and mixed reality headsets, software, integrations, and professional services for scalable training across air, land, and sea. 

([varjo.com](https://varjo.com/)) Varjo’s offering centers on the XR-4 Series, supported by Varjo Base software, broad software compatibility, and deployment-focused services for secure, mission-critical simulation.
